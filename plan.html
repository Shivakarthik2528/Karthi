About Our College

Where Education Goes Green

GreenHaven College is committed to creating an environmentally responsible campus. We believe students should learn not only from classrooms, but also from the environment around them.

Our campus combines modern education with sustainable practices including renewable energy, rainwater harvesting, tree plantation, recycling, organic gardening and environmental awareness.
